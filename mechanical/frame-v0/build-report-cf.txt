Frame geometry CANNOT improve this at a fixed wheelbase, and that is
worth stating as a derivation rather than an opinion:
  four motors sit on a circle of radius wheelbase/2. Adjacent tip gap
  is set by the SMALLEST adjacent chord. For a rectangle of half-width
  a and half-length b with 2*sqrt(a^2+b^2) = wheelbase, the adjacent
  spacings are 2a and 2b, so min(2a, 2b) is maximised at a = b --
  i.e. by the symmetric X this frame already is. A stretched or
  'dead-cat' layout makes one pair CLOSER, never further apart.
  Longer arms do not help either: they would increase the wheelbase,
  which is the fixed input.
